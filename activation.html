Which Activation Method Should You Choose?

GG Mouse Pro offers three activation methods to suit different needs:

  • Wireless: Best for convenience and flexibility. Activate from anywhere in your home.
  • Wired: Most reliable with lowest latency. Perfect for competitive gaming.
  • Device-to-Device: Ideal if you don't have a computer available.

General Activation Steps

  1. Download and install GG Mouse Pro from our download page.
  2. Launch the app on your Android device.
  3. Select your preferred activation method.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions for that method.
  5. Once activated, connect your keyboard and mouse.
  6. Start gaming!

Troubleshooting Activation Issues

If you're having trouble activating GG Mouse Pro, try these solutions:

  • Make sure your devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (for wireless activation).
  • Try a different USB cable or port (for wired activation).
  • Restart both your phone and computer/other device.
  • Check our FAQ for more solutions.
  • Contact our support team if you still need help.
